# Field capture protocol

## Mode A — connected property tour

For every room:

1. Stand close to the room centre, not directly against a wall.
2. Keep the phone upright at approximately chest height.
3. Start facing a textured wall or corner.
4. Choose one capture pattern per room:
   - **Quick Room View:** one level clockwise rotation.
   - **Full Room Sphere:** one upper-ring rotation, one lower-ring rotation, then ceiling and floor.
5. Follow auto-capture prompts; do not walk around the room while rotating.
6. Keep lighting and exposure stable.
7. Include the doorway used to enter/leave the room.
8. Capture the next room in physical walking order so the automatic room graph is meaningful.
9. Review the server QA result before leaving the property.

Avoid moving people, spinning fans, televisions, mirrors dominating the image, very dark rooms and rapid movement.

## Mode B — design scan

### Before scanning

1. Remove avoidable moving objects and turn on room lighting.
2. Create the full room list in physical order.
3. Keep a tape or laser meter available for trusted scale measurements.
4. On Depth-capable phones, confirm that the app reports RGB-D capture; otherwise continue with the AR/manual fallback.

### Guided scan

1. Scan one room at a time and keep the phone in portrait orientation.
2. Walk slowly around the perimeter rather than rotating only at the centre.
3. Keep the centre reticle on the surface or feature being recorded.
4. Show every floor/wall and wall/ceiling boundary.
5. Pause at each corner and tap **Corner**.
6. Approach doors, windows and open passages from useful angles and mark them.
7. Mark floor, ceiling, stairs or level changes where relevant.
8. Use **Measure A** and **Measure B** for AR endpoint proposals; confirm important values with tape or laser.
9. Follow the live prompts until walls, floor, ceiling, corners and openings have usable coverage.
10. Return near the starting position to reduce trajectory drift.

### Field-plan confirmation

1. Choose Rectangle, L-shape or Free plan.
2. Drag vertices to match the actual room.
3. Add every door, window and passage to its real wall using offset, width, height and sill.
4. Select the measurement method and tolerance.
5. Set floor ID, elevation, room origin and rotation.
6. Add a doorway/connection anchor when aligning adjacent rooms.
7. Save the field plan before uploading evidence.

### Evidence and handoff

1. Upload Capture Package v2.1 only after the field plan is confirmed.
2. The app verifies file checksums before upload.
3. Editing a plan after upload requires re-upload.
4. Create the draft project and continue correction in Designer Studio.
5. Do not publish or use the draft for fabrication until the required verification level is reached.

Do not claim that wall removal is structurally safe. Structural decisions require an appropriately qualified professional.
